A Manager, an Inspiring Personality, and a Professional Therapist

1. A Manager
A manager is a person who plans, organizes, directs, and controls the activities of an organization or group in order to achieve specific goals. A manager ensures that available resources such as people, money, time, and materials are used effectively and efficiently. The purpose of a manager is to provide leadership, coordinate teamwork, make sound decisions, solve problems, and ensure that organizational objectives are achieved successfully.

2. An Inspiring Personality
An inspiring personality is someone whose character, attitude, words, and actions motivate others to become better versions of themselves. Such a person influences people positively through integrity, courage, discipline, compassion, and determination. The purpose of an inspiring personality is to encourage hope, build confidence, promote positive values, and empower others to overcome challenges and pursue excellence in their personal, academic, professional, and spiritual lives.

3. A Professional Therapist
A professional therapist is a trained specialist who helps individuals, couples, families, or groups cope with emotional, psychological, behavioural, or relationship challenges. Through counselling, guidance, and evidence-based therapeutic techniques, a therapist assists people in improving their mental and emotional well-being. The purpose of a professional therapist is to provide emotional support, promote healing, develop healthy coping skills, improve relationships, and help individuals achieve a balanced and fulfilling life.

Conclusion
Although these three roles are different, they all contribute to the growth and well-being of individuals and society. A manager promotes organizational success, an inspiring personality motivates positive change, and a professional therapist supports emotional and mental wellness. Together, they play vital roles in building productive, healthy, and successful communities.

Business Registration & Management in Nigeria

Management is the process of planning, organizing, directing, and controlling the resources and activities of a business to achieve its objectives efficiently and effectively. For a business to operate legally in Nigeria, it must be registered and regulated under the appropriate laws.

The principal law governing the registration and operation of companies in Nigeria is the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020. This Act provides the legal framework for the formation, registration, management, and winding up of companies, business names, and incorporated trustees. It is administered by the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), which is responsible for registering businesses and ensuring compliance with the law.

Under CAMA, the major forms of business registration include:

Business Name (Sole Proprietorship or Partnership); Private Company Limited by Shares (Ltd); Public Limited Company (PLC); Company Limited by Guarantee; Incorporated Trustees (for churches, NGOs, clubs, and associations).

Other laws that regulate businesses in Nigeria include: the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (as amended); the Federal Inland Revenue Service (Establishment) Act; the Personal Income Tax Act and Companies Income Tax Act; the Value Added Tax (VAT) Act; the Labour Act; the Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission (NIPC) Act; and industry-specific laws for sectors such as banking, insurance, telecommunications, and oil and gas.

Business registration provides legal recognition, enhances credibility, protects the business name, enables access to loans and government contracts, and ensures compliance with Nigerian laws. Every entrepreneur should register and manage their business in accordance with the relevant legal provisions to promote sustainable growth and avoid legal sanctions.
